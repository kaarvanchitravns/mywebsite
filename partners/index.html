Partner with KAARVAN.

Offer the fleets you already serve tolls, tracking, safety and trip accounts — from one platform, with your name on the relationship.

What a partner gets

Dealers, fitment shops and channel partners issue KAARVAN FASTag and devices, and run their stock, activations and commission from a portal of their own. Terms and commission are set per partner.

Stock in your name

Devices and tags allocated to you, with a running record of what you hold and what you have issued.

Issue on the spot

Activate a device or a tag for a customer from the partner portal while you are standing at the vehicle.

Your own wallet

A partner wallet with its own balance, top-ups and a statement you can reconcile.

Commission you can check

Pricing per product, applied to each activation, and a ledger that shows how each figure was reached.

Your own team

Add your staff with their own logins and rights, so you are not sharing one password.

Activation support

When KYC or a device needs our side to act, it comes to us and not to your customer.

Enterprise and institutional partnerships

Beyond the dealer channel, KAARVAN is built to sit behind other people’s businesses. These conversations are open — we are not claiming any of them yet.

Banks and financial institutions

FASTag issuance runs with a banking partner, and the platform already handles KYC, wallets, ledgers and statements. The same rails suit lending against verified trip and toll history.

OEMs and bodybuilders

Fit tracking and tolling at the point of sale rather than after it, with activation handled through the partner portal.

Fleet aggregators and logistics platforms

Vehicle, trip and payment data through an API, so a platform can see the same thing its operators see.

Insurers and claims teams

Dashcam footage, trip history and driver-behaviour signals tied to one vehicle, for faster and better-evidenced claims.

If you are building something that needs any of this, write to sales@kaarvan.in and we will talk properly.
