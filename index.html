Four products, one connected ecosystem.

Most operators buy tolling from one company, tracking from another and a dashcam from a third, then keep the account of what each trip earned in a notebook. Four relationships, and no single place where a payment, a location and an expense describe the same vehicle.

KAARVAN is built the other way round. Each layer feeds the next, so the toll that was charged, the kilometres that were driven and the bill that was sent all belong to one trip, on one platform.
