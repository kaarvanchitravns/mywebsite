KAARVAN Toll Calculator

Know the toll before the truck leaves the yard.

Toll Calculator shows every toll plaza between any two places in India, what each one charges for your vehicle, and what the route costs you in a month.

How to start

  1. Pick the route. Type the city or town where the trip starts in From and choose it from the suggestions, then do the same for where it ends in To. Any two places in India work.
  2. Pick the vehicle class. Toll is charged by axles, not wheels. A 6-wheeler is a 2-axle truck, a 10-wheeler is 3-axle, a 12-, 14- or 16-wheeler is 4–6 axle, and long trailers with seven or more axles are 7+ axle.
  3. Pick the trip. Single for one way. Return in 24 h if the truck comes back through the same plazas within 24 hours, which is cheaper than two single trips.
  4. Read the toll. The total, every plaza on the way, the cost per km and the monthly cost for the trips you enter.
