KAARVAN FASTag

Intelligent Toll Payments

Keep moving. Keep paying.

Digital toll payments for commercial vehicles and fleets. One wallet for the whole fleet, every toll transaction recorded against the vehicle that made it, and recharge without a trip to the bank.

KAARVAN FASTag is issued by our partner bank under the NPCI FASTag programme. KAARVAN is the tech and service partner: we handle the fleet wallet, auto-pay per tag, and the support. The bank holds the tag and the funds.

Still in build. Tags are issued after KYC, so a tag is set up for your account rather than switched on by default. Tell us now and we will come to you the week it opens.
