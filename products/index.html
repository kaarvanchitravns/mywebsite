KAARVAN Safar Khata

Digital Trip Ledger

Every trip, every rupee.

A trip is created for a vehicle and every rupee spent on it is recorded against it. GPS supplies the kilometres, FASTag supplies the tolls, the driver adds bills from his phone, and the owner sees profit per trip, diesel mileage, each driver's balance and the position across every branch.

Trips move through planned, started, reached, completed and settled — and GPS can start and close them on its own when the vehicle leaves the origin and reaches the destination.

At the end of a trip it produces the receipt — a trip bill, a duty bill for a corporate client, a driver's hisab slip or a client's monthly statement — sent straight to WhatsApp with your UPI details on it.

The receipt at the end of the trip

Part of Safar Khata, and the part we are opening first — because it is what every operator still does by hand, and what a customer actually asks for.

Four kinds of bill

A trip bill for the customer, a duty bill for a corporate client, a hisab slip for the driver, and a monthly statement for a regular party — each made from the trip you already recorded.

Sent on WhatsApp, not photographed

The bill goes as a link your customer opens on his phone, not a picture of a paper slip. You can switch any shared link off afterwards.

Your UPI on every bill

Upload your UPI QR once and it rides on every bill you send, with a Pay button on it. The money comes to you, not through us.

Your customer can check it is real

Every customer bill carries an eight-character code. Anyone can verify it without signing in, so your bill stands up when a payment is queried.

The receipt your passenger asks for

Trip Bookings is not only for freight. If you drive your own cab or run a few of them, the bill at the end of the ride is the whole job.

A cab trip

Fare plus the charges that actually happen — toll, parking, night charge. The passenger gets a proper bill, not a figure said out loud at the end of the ride.

A corporate duty

Bill a package — say eight hours and eighty kilometres — then extra kilometres at your rate and extra time in half hours, worked out from the odometer and the clock rather than from memory.

Sent, and verifiable

The bill goes to WhatsApp with your UPI details and a Pay button on it, and carries a code your corporate client can check without signing in.

It counts towards your month

The billed total becomes that trip's freight, so your receivables, your statements and your profit stay right without a second entry.

How it is built

The decisions that matter once you are depending on it every day.

Separate services, on purpose

Tolling, tracking and the partner portal run as separate processes. A release of one, or a fault in one, does not take the others down — and there are tests that prove tracking serves no tolling routes.

Multi-tenant from the database up

Each account's data is isolated at the database level, not filtered in application code, with an isolation test suite that runs on every change.

Roles, rights and approvals

Owner, manager, dispatcher, viewer and driver each see a different platform. Changes that touch money or access need a second person to approve them.

Works where the signal doesn't

Entries made out of coverage queue on the device and sync when the network returns, so a driver is never blocked by a dead zone.

Live, not refreshed

Location and events arrive over a live connection, so the map and the trip move while you are watching them.

Switched on deliberately

Every product and feature has a switch. Nothing reaches an account until it has been built, tested and turned on for that account.
