About KAARVAN

Building the digital backbone of India's commercial vehicles.

Why we exist, how we think, and where the platform is headed.

Connected roads start with connected technology.
The problem

India's roads are connected. Its fleets should be too.

Most Indian fleets still run payments, tracking, safety and records through separate tools, spreadsheets and phone calls. KAARVAN brings them onto one platform built for commercial transport.
