Privacy notice

What we collect from this website, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can require us to do with it.

In force from 20 November 2026

KAARVAN operates kaarvan.in. This notice tells you what personal data the website collects, why, who it reaches, how long it is kept, and what you can require us to do with it. It covers this website only. If you become a customer, the terms of your service agreement and the notice given inside the KAARVAN app will apply to the data generated by your vehicles.

1. Who is responsible

KAARVAN is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here. For anything in this notice, write to sales@kaarvan.in.

2. What we collect, and why

We collect only what you type into a form on this website, and only for the purpose stated beside that form when you submit it.

  • Enquiry form — name, mobile number, email address, city, state, and whatever you write in the message. Purpose: to answer your enquiry and, where you have asked about a product, to contact you about it.
  • Idea and note forms — name and the note you write. Purpose: to read your suggestion, to reply if a reply is needed, and, only if you have given us permission in writing, to publish the note on this website.

We do not ask for, and you should not send us through this website, any financial details, identity documents, vehicle registration documents or KYC papers.

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and tracks no one across the internet. Two technical disclosures are owed to you all the same: our pages load the IBM Plex typeface from Google’s font servers, which means your IP address reaches Google when a page loads; and our hosting provider keeps ordinary server logs of requests, as every web host does.

3. Consent, and how to withdraw it

We process this data on your consent, which you give by submitting the form. Consent is free, specific and informed: you choose whether to write to us, you are told the purpose before you send it, and we use it for nothing else.

You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing sales@kaarvan.in with the word WITHDRAW in the subject line. It is as easy to withdraw as it was to give. On withdrawal we stop processing and erase your data, unless a law requires us to keep it. Withdrawal does not make unlawful anything done before you withdrew.

4. Who else sees it

  • Our form service. Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by a third-party form provider acting on our instructions. It transmits the message; it does not use your data for its own purposes.
  • Our email provider, which holds the message once it arrives.
  • Our hosting provider, which serves these pages.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it for advertising. We disclose it outside KAARVAN only where a law, a court or a lawful authority requires it, and we tell you when we are permitted to tell you.

5. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become business: erased within 24 months of our last contact with you.
  • Enquiries that become business: retained under your service agreement, and for as long afterwards as tax and company law require.
  • Notes and ideas: erased within 24 months, unless published with your permission, in which case we keep the note and the name you gave for as long as it stays on the site.

6. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data of yours we hold and who we have shared it with;
  • correct, complete or update anything inaccurate;
  • erase your data, where no law requires us to keep it;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapable of exercising them yourself.

Write to sales@kaarvan.in. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act, so that we do not hand your data to someone else.

7. Grievances

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, write to sales@kaarvan.in marked GRIEVANCE. We will acknowledge within 7 working days and answer within 90 days.

If our answer does not satisfy you, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India. Nothing in this notice takes that right away.

8. Security

We apply reasonable security safeguards to the data we hold, including access control on the inbox that receives enquiries. No transmission over the internet is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify each affected person and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

9. Children

This website is for people running or buying commercial vehicles, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect the personal data of anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us data, write to us and we will erase it.

10. Changes

If we change this notice we will publish the new version here and change the date at the top. Where a change materially affects how we use data you have already given us, we will ask for your consent again rather than rely on the old one.
